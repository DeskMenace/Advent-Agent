# The Time Capsule

A letter from you about who they are this December, sealed in a capsule. It opens fully only on a date they choose next year. Until then, it shows how long is left.

## Make it theirs

- The letter is specific and true.

## Great vs lazy

**Great:** They can add their own message before sealing.

**Lazy:** A letter that opens immediately.
