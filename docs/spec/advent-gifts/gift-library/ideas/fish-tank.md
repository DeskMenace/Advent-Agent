# The Fish Tank

A glass aquarium. Each fish is named after a person, pet or in-joke in their life and behaves accordingly. Tap the glass, drop food, find the hidden treasure chest.

## Make it theirs

- Fish personalities are drawn from real people and pets ("Biscuit" the fish sulks at the filter).
- As a collection, each new gift adds one fish to the same tank.

## Great vs lazy

**Great:** Bubbles, caustic light, fish that remember who was fed.

**Lazy:** Fish clipart moving in straight lines.
