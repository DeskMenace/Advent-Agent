# Something in the Envelope

A 3D envelope with a wax seal. Break the seal; what comes out is unexpected — a letter that unfolds into a map, a pressed flower, a ticket to an imaginary place, a tiny origami animal.

## Make it theirs

- The object inside relates to them (an edelweiss pressed between pages for a mountain person).
- The seal carries their initial or a symbol of theirs.

## Great vs lazy

**Great:** The object itself is interactive (the map unfolds panel by panel).

**Lazy:** An envelope that opens to a block of text.
