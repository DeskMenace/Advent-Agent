# The Crossword of Your Year

A proper crossword where every clue is about their life, their people and their in-jokes. Solving it reveals a hidden message in the shaded squares.

## Make it theirs

- Every clue is about them; the hidden message is personal.

## Great vs lazy

**Great:** Clever, punny clues; the hidden message lands.

**Lazy:** A word search.
