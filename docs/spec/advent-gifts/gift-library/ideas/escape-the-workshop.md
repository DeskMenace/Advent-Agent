# Escape the Workshop

They're locked in their Secret Santa's workshop on Christmas Eve. The puzzles use things only they know and the gifts from this season. The last lock opens to the final present.

## Make it theirs

- Codes and riddles draw on their real life and previous gifts (the plaque title from Day 1, the crossword answer from Day 9).
- The final room reveals a personal message.

## Great vs lazy

**Great:** Every puzzle has an "aha", hints are in character, and the ending lands.

**Lazy:** A quiz with a locked-door image.
