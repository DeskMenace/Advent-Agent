# Snow Globe of Your Year

A glass snow globe on a little wooden base. Shake it (drag, swipe or shake the phone) and tiny objects from their year swirl with the snow, then settle into a scene.

## Make it theirs

- The scene inside is their place (their city skyline, their mountain, their street), not a generic village.
- The swirling objects are 5–8 real things from their year (the dog, the laptop, the ice axe, the K-drama remote), drawn simply in the same style.
- The engraved plaque on the base carries their name and a one-line title for their year.
- Hidden layer — shake hard three times and one object stays floating with a tiny caption.

## Great vs lazy

**Great:** Glass has a highlight and slight refraction, snow drifts with inertia, objects tumble at different speeds and settle where they belong in the scene. The plaque title made them laugh.

**Lazy:** A static picture of a snow globe with their name on it.
