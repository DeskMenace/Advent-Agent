# The Bracket of Their Favourite Things

A sports-style tournament bracket of 16 of their favourite things (foods, places, shows, habits). They pick winners round by round; the champion gets an absurdly grand trophy ceremony.

## Make it theirs

- All 16 contenders are theirs; commentary is in the style of a sports announcer who knows them.

## Great vs lazy

**Great:** Commentary reacts to upsets.

**Lazy:** A poll.
