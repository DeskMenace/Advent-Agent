Numbers are the gift; the captions are the charm. Compute every figure from real data and say where it came from.
Great: story-style cards, bold typography, a genuinely surprising stat ("you wrote 40% of your code on Tuesdays") and a final "top 5 files" reveal.
Lazy: made-up percentages in a template, or a plain table.
Pitfall: leaking repo names, client names or colleagues; aggregate or anonymise anything that is not the recipient's own.
