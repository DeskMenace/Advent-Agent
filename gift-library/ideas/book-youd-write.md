The cover should look like it belongs in their favourite bookshop section.
Great: a moody cover for "The Cartographer of Small Hours", a blurb quoting a fictional reviewer, and a first chapter that ends on a hook.
Lazy: a title on a gradient and two paragraphs.
Pitfall: imitating a real author's protected work or using a real author's name on the quotes.
