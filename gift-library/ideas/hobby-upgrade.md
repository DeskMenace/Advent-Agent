Compatibility is the research: the right thread size, the right format, the right model.
Great: a specific replacement pour-over filter holder that fits the recipient's exact kettle size, with vendor, price, date and a note on why paper vs metal.
Lazy: "Buy better headphones."
Pitfall: recommending gear that does not fit what they own.
