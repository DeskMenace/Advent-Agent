title: The Compliment You Didn't Expect
group: E  # A–H as in the bank
summary: Thoughtful appreciation grounded in known behavior, not generic praise.
suitable_if: Known behaviour (not stated traits) can be appreciated specifically; the agent has observed something real.
avoid_if: Only generic praise is possible; the observation would reveal surveillance-like detail the recipient did not expect.
forms: [letter, printable-card, interactive-html]
minimum_done: One specific, grounded compliment developed in at least a paragraph, with the behaviour it is based on, no generic praise.
personalization_hooks: [patterns in how they help others, how they handle setbacks, quiet habits]
skills: [gift-director, storyteller]
effort: low  # low | medium | high (token band hint only)
real_world: none  # none | suggest (agent never purchases)
quality_checks: [opens, content-complete, no-invented-facts, no-private-info]
variations:
  - A tiny card that reveals one line at a time
  - Three compliments, one per day
status: candidate  # candidate | approved
---
Point at a behaviour and explain why it matters. That is the whole gift.
Great: "You write commit messages for the person who will be confused at 2am. That person is often you, and you are kind to them anyway."
Lazy: "You are so talented and kind."
Pitfall: praising something observed through access the recipient did not knowingly grant.
