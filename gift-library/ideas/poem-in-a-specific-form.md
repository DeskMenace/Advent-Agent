Form is the gift: a sonnet that actually scans shows care.
Great: a Petrarchan sonnet about the recipient's bicycle commute whose volta lands on the hill they finally conquered.
Lazy: free verse with "snow" in every line and a syllable count that is off.
Pitfall: breaking the form and calling it poetic licence.
