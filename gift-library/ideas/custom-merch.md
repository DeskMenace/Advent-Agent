title: Custom Merch
group: H  # A–H as in the bank
summary: Real merch design plus usable print-on-demand vendor or ready-to-upload production asset.
suitable_if: A design idea rooted in the recipient's interests exists and a print-on-demand vendor can be named with a usable path.
avoid_if: The design would use third-party IP; no vendor ships to the recipient; the price ceiling is too low for merch.
forms: [image-set, real-world-proposal, production-asset]
minimum_done: An original print-ready design (correct size, colour profile noted), a mockup, and either a named vendor with price and date checked or a ready-to-upload production asset; the recipient orders.
personalization_hooks: [catchphrases, project logos (their own), favourite colours]
skills: [gift-director, art-studio, concierge]
effort: medium  # low | medium | high (token band hint only)
real_world: suggest  # none | suggest (agent never purchases)
quality_checks: [opens, content-complete, print-sizes-correct, links-verified, price-date-checked, no-purchase-claim]
variations:
  - Tote bag with a one-line poem
  - A tiny run of stickers
status: candidate  # candidate | approved
---
Production-ready means the vendor's spec: size, bleed, DPI, colour mode.
Great: a vector tote design in two inks, a mockup, a vendor that ships to the recipient, the price for one bag and the date checked.
Lazy: a low-res PNG and "put it on a shirt".
Pitfall: implying the order was placed.
