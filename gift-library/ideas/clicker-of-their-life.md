# The Idle Clicker of Their Life

An absurd idle game where they produce the thing they produce most (commits, summits, cups of tea). Upgrades are real parts of their life, and the numbers get ridiculous.

## Make it theirs

- The resource and the upgrades are their life, escalating into the absurd.

## Great vs lazy

**Great:** News ticker with fake headlines about their empire.

**Lazy:** A counter.
