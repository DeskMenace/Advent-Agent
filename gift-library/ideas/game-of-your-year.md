# The Game of Your Year

A well-designed little game where they (or their pet) travel through their year, level by level. The collectibles are this season's gifts; the final level ends on a real celebration.

## Make it theirs

- Each level is a chapter of their year (a place, a project, a season).
- Enemies are their real nemeses, played for laughs (the flaky test, the 6 am alarm, the uphill).
- Collectibles are callbacks to gifts they opened this month; collections they completed appear as power-ups.
- The end screen is a touching message and a confetti burst.

## Great vs lazy

**Great:** It feels designed, with juice (screenshake, particles, satisfying jumps), a soundtrack, a secret level, and an ending that makes them a bit emotional.

**Lazy:** A one-screen game with rectangles and their name in the title.
