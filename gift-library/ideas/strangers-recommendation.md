The reason is the gift. "Because you loved X and this does the same thing sideways."
Great: a handwritten-style card recommending a 1960s novella because of the recipient's love for a particular film's silences, with a library link.
Lazy: a bestseller list.
Pitfall: recommending something that does not exist; verify title, creator and availability.
