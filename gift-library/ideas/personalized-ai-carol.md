An original carol means an original melody too, not new words on a traditional tune.
Great: a three-verse carol about the recipient's street in December, music-box melody, printable sheet with chords.
Lazy: "Jingle Bells" with the recipient's name inserted.
Pitfall: naming people who did not agree to appear.
