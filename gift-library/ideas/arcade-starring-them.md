# An Arcade Game Starring Them

A small arcade game where they (or their pet) are the hero and their real nemeses are the enemies. Ends on a shareable high-score card.

## Make it theirs

- Hero, enemies, power-ups and level names from their life.

## Great vs lazy

**Great:** Juice (particles, screenshake, sound), difficulty that ramps, rank titles that are jokes about them.

**Lazy:** A clone of Snake with a name swap.
