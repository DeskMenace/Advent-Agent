A generator is a gift when it removes the boring 80% and leaves the fun part.
Great: a one-click weekly menu planner that honours the recipient's dietary notes, prints to one page and remembers nothing.
Lazy: a form that echoes its inputs.
Pitfall: storing personal inputs anywhere but memory.
