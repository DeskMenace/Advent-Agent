Playful means optional. No streaks, no guilt, no notifications.
Great: "Today, notice five blue things. Tap each when you find them." with a tracker that ends in a small drawing of all five.
Lazy: "Drink 2 litres of water" with a progress bar.
Pitfall: anything that resembles a dark pattern.
