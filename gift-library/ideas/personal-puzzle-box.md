title: Personal Puzzle Box
group: B  # A–H as in the bank
summary: Printable puzzle or browser puzzle with several clues and final hidden message.
suitable_if: Several small shared facts or interests exist to build clues from; the recipient likes solving things.
avoid_if: Clues would require private facts; the recipient finds puzzles frustrating.
forms: [interactive-html, printable-puzzle]
minimum_done: At least three solvable clues leading to a final hidden message; a working check mechanism or a printed answer key sealed on the last page.
personalization_hooks: [shared facts, favourite numbers and words, places]
skills: [gift-director, arcade, storyteller]
effort: medium  # low | medium | high (token band hint only)
real_world: none  # none | suggest (agent never purchases)
quality_checks: [opens, interaction-works, content-complete, no-private-info]
variations:
  - A cipher wheel to print and cut out
  - A box that opens over several days, one lock per door
status: candidate  # candidate | approved
---
Clues must be fair: solvable from the gift itself plus things the recipient surely knows.
Great: three layered puzzles (a cipher, a picture riddle, a word ladder) whose answers combine into a code that unlocks a heartfelt message.
Lazy: a single "what is your name?" question gating a paragraph.
Pitfall: an unsolvable step; include a hint mechanism.
