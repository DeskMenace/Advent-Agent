Only use landmarks you can verify exist; the riddles can be whimsical, the route cannot.
Great: a 3 km loop with riddles at a mural, a fountain and a bakery, a map, a weather note and a final "read this on the bench" card.
Lazy: "Go for a walk and look around."
Pitfall: routes through unsafe or closed areas; check opening hours and winter conditions.
