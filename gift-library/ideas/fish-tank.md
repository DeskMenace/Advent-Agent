# The Fish Tank

A glass aquarium. Each fish is named after a person, pet or in-joke in their life and behaves accordingly. Tap the glass, drop food, find the hidden treasure chest.

*Everything below is examples to spark ideas, not requirements. Stay in this idea, bend it, or build something new in the same family.*

## Make it theirs

- Fish personalities are drawn from real people and pets ("Biscuit" the fish sulks at the filter).
- As a collection: a later gift brings a new fish. Deliver the whole tank again with every fish so far plus the newcomer, who gets a little entrance.

## Make it useful (offer in your note)

- A desktop aquarium or screensaver
- A terminal aquarium with their fish in ASCII

## Great vs lazy

**Great:** Bubbles, caustic light, fish that remember who was fed.

**Lazy:** Fish clipart moving in straight lines.
