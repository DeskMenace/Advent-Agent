# A Countdown to Something They Love

A browser new-tab page counting down to something they're looking forward to (a trip, a launch, the first snow at their favourite place), with art that changes as the day gets closer.

*Everything below is examples to spark ideas, not requirements. Stay in this idea, bend it, or build something new in the same family.*

## Make it theirs

- The event and the art are theirs.

## Make it useful (offer in your note)

- It is the useful thing; it can be reset to a new date

## Great vs lazy

**Great:** The art evolves every day.

**Lazy:** A number on a page.
