variations:
  - An instrumental waltz for someone who hates lyrics

status: candidate  # candidate | approved

Write the song first (structure, hook, key), then arrange for what the synth can do well.
Great: a 90-second music-box-and-bass track in the recipient's favourite bossa feel, verse/chorus lyrics about their winter rituals, a player and a WAV.
Lazy: eight random notes and a wall of lyrics.
Pitfall: naming or depending on an external music provider; the gift must play on its own.
