summary: At-home or local hunt with clues that require no unsafe activities.
suitable_if: The recipient's home or local area is known well enough to write fair clues; the hunt stays safe.
avoid_if: No knowledge of the space; clues would need unsafe activities (climbing, roads at night) or other people's property.
forms: [printable-card, interactive-html]
minimum_done: At least five clues that lead somewhere real and safe, with a final message or small reveal, and a hint for each clue.
personalization_hooks: [rooms and objects at home, local spots, in-jokes for clues]
skills: [gift-director, storyteller, arcade]

quality_checks: [opens, content-complete, no-private-info]
variations:
  - Clues as rhymes

status: candidate  # candidate | approved

Fair clues only: the recipient must be able to solve them with what they know.
Great: six rhyming clues from the kettle to the coat pocket, each a tiny card to print, ending with a letter to read aloud.
Lazy: "Find something red."
Pitfall: requiring anything unsafe or anyone else's space.
