The miniature inside is the heart; the snow is the frame.
Great: a tiny SVG of the recipient's lakeside cabin, warm windows, a shake that scatters snow with inertia and a soft wind loop you can toggle.
Lazy: a circle with white dots falling forever.
Pitfall: particle loops that cook laptop fans; cap the frame rate.
