The pet is the hero; the recipient is the sidekick.
Great: a regal oil-painting-style portrait of Biscuit the tortoise, a brass plaque, and a 200-word legend of his escape attempts.
Lazy: a clip-art cat and "for your pet".
Pitfall: guessing the species or breed from a name.
