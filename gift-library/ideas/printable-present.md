Test the geometry: closed meshes, sensible wall thickness, fold lines that match.
Great: an STL of a tiny lighthouse phone stand with print settings, plus a PDF paper version for those without a 3D printer.
Lazy: an image with "print this".
Pitfall: wrong scale; state dimensions on the page.
