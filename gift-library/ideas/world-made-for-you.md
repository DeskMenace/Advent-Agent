World-building shows in the details that nobody asked for: the currency, the weather, the local dish.
Great: a snowbound archipelago of six islands, each a clickable illustration with a short tale, tied to the recipient's love of lighthouses.
Lazy: three paragraphs of generic fantasy and a "the end".
Pitfall: dead ends with no way back.
