# The Music Box

A wooden music box. Turn the crank and a melody composed for them plays while a tiny figure (their pet, them on skis) turns on top.

*Everything below is examples to spark ideas, not requirements. Stay in this idea, bend it, or build something new in the same family.*

## Make it theirs

- The figure is them or their pet.
- The melody's title and engraving are theirs.

## Make it useful (offer in your note)

- A ringtone, alarm or notification sound

## Great vs lazy

**Great:** The pin cylinder visibly plucks the comb; it slows down when they stop cranking.

**Lazy:** A play button.
