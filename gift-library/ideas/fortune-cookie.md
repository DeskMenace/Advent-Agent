The crack animation and the writing carry it equally.
Great: a crunchy two-stage crack, a paper slip that unfurls, and fortunes like "The thing you keep postponing will take twenty minutes."
Lazy: a button that shows a random quote from the internet.
Pitfall: fortunes that pretend to know the future about anything serious.
