# A Festive Terminal

A Christmas theme for their terminal: colours, a prompt with a tiny tree that grows a decoration each day, a countdown to the 24th, and a greeting when they open a new tab.

*Everything below is examples to spark ideas, not requirements. Stay in this idea, bend it, or build something new in the same family.*

## Make it theirs

- Their name, their pet or their mountain in the greeting art.
- Colours from their taste.

## Make it useful (offer in your note)

- It is the useful thing; offer a non-festive version to keep after December

## Great vs lazy

**Great:** Tiny surprises: snow on Fridays, a different greeting each day.

**Lazy:** Only a colour scheme.
