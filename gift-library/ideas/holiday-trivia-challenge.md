Facts must be true. Prefer questions about the recipient's favourites over obscure general knowledge.
Great: a themed round per interest, a difficulty ramp and a shareable (local) scorecard.
Lazy: twenty generic "what colour is Rudolph's nose" questions.
Pitfall: confidently wrong trivia; verify anything you are not sure about or drop it.
