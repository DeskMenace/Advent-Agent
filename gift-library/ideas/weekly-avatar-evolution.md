A callback gift: the pleasure is recognition plus novelty.
Great: week one on a snowy balcony, week two in a ski lodge, each with one tiny continuity detail (the same lost mitten).
Lazy: the same image with a recoloured hat.
Pitfall: breaking continuity with a different art style.
