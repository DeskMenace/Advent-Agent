Melodrama is the engine: operatic language for a tiny grievance.
Great: a three-page comic in which "The Buffering" is born the night a video call froze for the seventh time, complete with a lair made of ethernet cables.
Lazy: a paragraph that says "and so you became evil" without any real detail.
Pitfall: making a real person the cause of the villainy.
