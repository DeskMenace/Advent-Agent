The tone is a fond, slightly pompous curator. Each exhibit is honoured, not mocked.
Great: a scrollable gallery with room names ("Hall of Sourdough"), framed SVG artefacts and labels like "Acquired 2023, provenance: a long weekend".
Lazy: a bulleted list of things the recipient quit.
Pitfall: inventing projects to fill the rooms; three real exhibits beat eight imagined ones.
