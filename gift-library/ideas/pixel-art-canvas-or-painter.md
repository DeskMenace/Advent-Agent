Make it feel like a toy, not a utility: chunky pixels, delightful palette, instant export.
Great: 32×32 canvas, winter palette, undo, mirror mode, and a pre-loaded sketch of the recipient's bicycle to finish.
Lazy: a canvas where clicks paint black squares and nothing else.
Pitfall: export that silently fails on mobile; test the download.
