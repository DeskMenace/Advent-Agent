Specific beats sentimental. One real observation is worth ten "you are amazing".
Great: a cream card with a hand-drawn sprig, a note that recalls the exact morning the recipient helped a neighbour, signed by the agent.
Lazy: "Happy Holidays! Hope you have a great season!" in a cursive font.
Pitfall: invented memories; if the agent does not know it, it does not write it.
