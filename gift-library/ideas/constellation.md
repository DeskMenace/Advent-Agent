# The Constellation of Your Year

A night sky. Each star is a moment from their year. Connect them in order and the lines draw a constellation in the shape of something they love.

## Make it theirs

- Stars are real moments; the final shape is their pet, mountain or symbol.

## Great vs lazy

**Great:** Shooting stars, a name for the constellation in pseudo-Latin.

**Lazy:** A starry image with text.
