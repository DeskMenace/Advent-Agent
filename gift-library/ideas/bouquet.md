# A Bouquet That Blooms

A bouquet of closed buds wrapped in paper. Each flower blooms when touched, and the card explains why each flower was chosen for them.

## Make it theirs

- Flowers they love or tied to their places (alpine flowers for a hiker). Each bouquet in the collection uses different flowers.
- The card uses the language of flowers, rewritten for them.

## Great vs lazy

**Great:** Petals unfold with variation, a few petals fall, the third bouquet arranges all three in a vase.

**Lazy:** An image of flowers.
