Verify the book exists exactly as named; invented titles are the classic failure.
Great: a bookseller's letter recommending a real, slightly obscure essay collection because of the recipient's love of long walks, with library and shop links checked on a stated date.
Lazy: "You might like a book about walking."
Pitfall: hallucinated titles or ISBNs.
