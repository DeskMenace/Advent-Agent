Catchy and short: a hook you can hum after one listen.
Great: a 15-second brass-and-bells sting celebrating the finished bookshelf, lyrics on screen in time with the notes.
Lazy: a scale with words typed underneath.
Pitfall: making a big deal out of something the recipient considers small or private.
