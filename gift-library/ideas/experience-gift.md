title: The Experience Gift
group: H  # A–H as in the bank
summary: Relevant workshop, exhibit, lesson or experience with bookable link and actual conditions.
suitable_if: Interests and location are known; a relevant workshop, exhibit, lesson or experience can be verified with a bookable link and real conditions.
avoid_if: Location unknown; the experience is sold out, out of season or beyond the price ceiling; mobility constraints are not met.
forms: [real-world-proposal, printable-card]
minimum_done: A specific experience with venue, dates or booking windows, price, conditions, bookable link, date checked and what is left for the recipient; they book.
personalization_hooks: [things they want to learn, exhibitions in their city, skills they admire]
skills: [gift-director, concierge, storyteller]
effort: medium  # low | medium | high (token band hint only)
real_world: suggest  # none | suggest (agent never purchases)
quality_checks: [opens, content-complete, links-verified, price-date-checked, no-purchase-claim]
variations:
  - A free experience (open day, guided walk)
  - An experience for two
status: candidate  # candidate | approved
---
Conditions matter: dates, group size, what to bring, cancellation terms.
Great: a Saturday pottery taster in the recipient's city with the next three dates, price, what is included, the booking link and a note on parking.
Lazy: "Take a class sometime."
Pitfall: stale dates or a link to a general homepage.
