Feel is the whole gift: easing, sound, haptics-like feedback.
Great: bubble wrap with per-bubble pop sounds, a counter that comments at milestones and a "new sheet" button.
Lazy: squares that change colour on click with no feedback.
Pitfall: autoplaying audio; sound starts only after the first tap.
