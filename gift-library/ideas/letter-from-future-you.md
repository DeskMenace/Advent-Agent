---
id: letter-from-future-you
title: A Letter from Future You
group: E  # A–H as in the bank
summary: Fictional, kind letter from the person in a chosen future year, such as December 2027.
suitable_if: Hopes, plans or values the recipient has shared can be gently projected into a chosen future year.
avoid_if: The recipient is anxious about the future or the only known plans are uncertain and sensitive (health, jobs).
forms: [letter, printable-card]
minimum_done: A complete, kind letter dated in the future (e.g. December 2027) that references real present-day details and makes no concrete predictions.
personalization_hooks: [stated hopes, current projects, a place they want to live or visit]
skills: [gift-director, storyteller]
effort: low  # low | medium | high (token band hint only)
real_world: none  # none | suggest (agent never purchases)
quality_checks: [opens, content-complete, no-invented-facts, no-private-info]
variations:
  - A letter from the recipient at 80
  - A postcard instead of a letter, from a future trip
status: candidate  # candidate | approved
---
Kindness, not prophecy. Future You reassures and notices, rather than reporting outcomes.
Great: "You still have the blue kettle. The thing you worried about in December 2026 turned out to be a Tuesday."
Lazy: "By 2027 you will be rich and famous."
Pitfall: predicting specifics about health, relationships or money.
