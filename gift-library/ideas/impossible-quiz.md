Quirky means the questions themselves are jokes, not just the options.
Great: "What was the real reason the 2024 road trip took eleven hours?" with four plausible answers and a feedback line that remembers the detour.
Lazy: generic pub trivia with the recipient's name in the title.
Pitfall: half-remembered facts presented as true; mark anything uncertain as a joke, or leave it out.
