A theme turns a shopping list into a story: "the rainy Sunday box".
Great: an illustrated box that opens to reveal five items with reasons, each with vendor, price and date checked, and a total against the ceiling.
Lazy: five random links.
Pitfall: suggesting the box is on its way.
