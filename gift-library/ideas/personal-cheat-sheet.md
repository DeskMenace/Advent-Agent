Accuracy is non-negotiable; layout is the gift wrap.
Great: a beautiful A4 card of sourdough hydration ratios and timings for the recipient's kitchen temperature, verified against two sources.
Lazy: a copied table from a wiki.
Pitfall: unchecked facts in a reference meant to be trusted.
