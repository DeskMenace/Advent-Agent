# A Focus Timer With Opinions

A small focus timer (terminal or browser page) where someone they love keeps time: their pet tells them to take a break, go outside, drink water.

*Everything below is examples to spark ideas, not requirements. Stay in this idea, bend it, or build something new in the same family.*

## Make it theirs

- The voice and the break suggestions are theirs (a walk with the dog, a view of their mountain).

## Make it useful (offer in your note)

- It is the useful thing

## Great vs lazy

**Great:** Little reactions if they skip a break.

**Lazy:** A plain countdown.
