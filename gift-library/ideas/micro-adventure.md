Whimsy on top, logistics underneath.
Great: "The Quest for the Frozen Lantern": a verified viewpoint reachable by bus 12, sunrise time, a thermos checklist and an illustrated map.
Lazy: "Visit a park near you."
Pitfall: stale opening hours; always print the date you checked.
