title: The Annual Roast
group: A  # A–H as in the bank
summary: A playful roast drawn from known habits, recurring events or opted-in work context; no coding requirement.
suitable_if: The agent knows real, harmless habits, recurring events or opted-in work context to tease; the recipient enjoys being teased.
avoid_if: No real context (an invented roast is just an insult); the known material touches health, money, relationships or anything the recipient has not shared on purpose.
forms: [interactive-html, letter, printable-card, slide-deck]
minimum_done: A complete roast with at least five specific, affectionate jabs that each point at something real, ending on genuine warmth.
personalization_hooks: [recurring habits, running jokes, calendar patterns, opted-in work context]
skills: [gift-director, storyteller]
effort: low  # low | medium | high (token band hint only)

quality_checks: [opens, content-complete, no-private-info, no-invented-facts]
variations:
  - "A \"roast by committee\": several fictional characters each take one jab"
  - A one-page awards ceremony with absurd categories
status: candidate  # candidate | approved

Punch at habits, never at the person. Every jab should make the recipient think "fair" before they laugh.
Great: a five-act roast where each act quotes a real habit (the 11pm commits, the fourth coffee) and the finale flips into sincere praise.
Lazy: generic "you drink too much coffee" lines that would fit anyone.
Pitfall: pulling in context the recipient did not opt in to; stick to what was shared for this purpose.
