Pick one thread and follow it; a story is not a list of months.
Great: a cosy tale of a fox who kept returning to the same hill (the recipient's running route) until the view changed, closing on this December.
Lazy: "In January you did X. In February you did Y."
Pitfall: dramatising events the recipient would rather forget.
