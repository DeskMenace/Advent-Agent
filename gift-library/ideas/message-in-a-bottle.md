# Message in a Bottle

A bottle bobbing on a gentle sea. Pull the cork, tip the bottle, and a rolled letter slides out and unrolls.

## Make it theirs

- The letter is from someone unexpected — their younger self, their pet, their favourite mountain — and is grounded in real details.
- The sea is their lake or coastline.

## Great vs lazy

**Great:** Light reflections on the water, a seagull with opinions.

**Lazy:** An image of a bottle and a letter below.
