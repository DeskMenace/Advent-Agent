# Snow in the Terminal

A `snow` command that makes it snow in their terminal. The snow piles up at the bottom and slowly forms the silhouette of something they love.

*Everything below is examples to spark ideas, not requirements. Stay in this idea, bend it, or build something new in the same family.*

## Make it theirs

- The shape that forms in the drift is theirs (their dog, their mountain, their initials).

## Make it useful (offer in your note)

- A screensaver mode; run it automatically on the 24th

## Great vs lazy

**Great:** Wind gusts, flakes of different sizes, the shape revealed slowly.

**Lazy:** Random asterisks.
