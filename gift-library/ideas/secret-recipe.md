The backstory is fiction; the recipe is not. Quantities must add up.
Great: "Grandmother Nobody's Midnight Ginger Cake" with a legend about a snowed-in bakery, a real tested ratio and a note on substitutions.
Lazy: a recipe copied from the web with a new name.
Pitfall: unsafe instructions or ignoring a known allergy.
