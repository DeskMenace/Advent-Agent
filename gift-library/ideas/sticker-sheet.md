# A Sticker Sheet Made for Them

A die-cut sticker sheet in a style they'd love. Peel each sticker off and slap it onto the page (or their "laptop lid"). Includes print-ready files and real print services.

*Everything below is examples to spark ideas, not requirements. Stay in this idea, bend it, or build something new in the same family.*

## Make it theirs

- 8–12 stickers of their pet, their phrases, their mountains, their inside jokes.

## Make it useful (offer in your note)

- Real stickers through a print service
- A sticker pack for their messaging app (transparent PNGs)

## Great vs lazy

**Great:** Holographic and matte finishes, satisfying peel physics.

**Lazy:** Images of stickers.
