The gap between tiny achievement and enormous celebration is the joke, and the warmth.
Great: fireworks, a marching band of SVG penguins and a plaque reading "Returned the library book on time, 14 November 2026".
Lazy: confetti and "Congratulations!" with nothing specific.
Pitfall: celebrating something the recipient is embarrassed about.
