# A Tiny Companion

A small animated character of their pet (or a creature inspired by them) lives on the page. It watches the cursor, reacts to clicks, falls asleep if ignored, and has one secret trick.

## Make it theirs

- The character is their real pet (breed, colour, quirks), or a creature built from their interests.
- Reactions come from real behaviour ("huffs at the vacuum", "zoomies after the snow").
- The secret trick is triggered by typing a word that matters to them.

## Great vs lazy

**Great:** Squash-and-stretch, blinking, ears that lag behind the head. People film it on their phone.

**Lazy:** A static drawing that bounces on click.
