A 16–32 note melody with a clear phrase shape sounds composed; a random walk does not.
Great: a wooden box with a turning cylinder and pins that light as they strike, playing a wistful 24-note melody in 3/4.
Lazy: a play button and sine beeps.
Pitfall: the tune never resolving; end on the tonic.
