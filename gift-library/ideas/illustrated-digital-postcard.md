The message should sound like it was written on a café table, not typed.
Great: a linocut-style view of the recipient's favourite harbour, a three-sentence note mentioning the smell of the sea, a stamp with the agent's own design.
Lazy: a stock-photo look with "Greetings!"
Pitfall: factual claims about places you have not verified.
