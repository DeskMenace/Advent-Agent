id: personal-dashboard
title: Your Personal Dashboard
group: F  # A–H as in the bank
summary: Tiny dashboard about a permitted project, hobby or personal interest.
suitable_if: A permitted project, hobby or interest has data the recipient agreed to use (reading log, garden notes, training).
avoid_if: No permitted data; the dashboard would need live credentials or remote services.
forms: [mini-app, interactive-html]
minimum_done: A tiny dashboard with real (snapshot) data, at least three views, legible charts and a note on where the data came from.
personalization_hooks: [hobby logs, reading lists, training notes, plant watering]
skills: [gift-director, maker, art-studio]
effort: medium  # low | medium | high (token band hint only)
real_world: none  # none | suggest (agent never purchases)
quality_checks: [opens, content-complete, no-invented-facts, no-private-info]
variations:
  - "A paper-style \"annual report\" of the hobby"
  - A sparkline postcard
status: candidate  # candidate | approved
---
Make it warm: paper textures and handwritten labels, not a corporate BI tool.
Great: a reading dashboard from the recipient's shared book list, pages per month, favourite genres, and a "next up" shelf.
Lazy: a bar chart of numbers nobody asked for.
Pitfall: embedding tokens or calling remote APIs from the gift page.
