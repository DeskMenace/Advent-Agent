Design for enamel: bold shapes, few colours, metal lines, 25–32 mm.
Great: a 30 mm hard-enamel kettle with steam in two colours, a photoreal mockup on denim, a spec sheet, a vendor with a 10-piece minimum and the cost estimate checked on a stated date.
Lazy: a detailed painting that could never be a pin.
Pitfall: a design that does not survive the medium, or any claim that it was ordered.
