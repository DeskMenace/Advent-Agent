A recipe gift is a promise of an evening; make the steps foolproof.
Great: a one-page braised leek and lentil dish for the recipient's vegetarian household, with a spiced pear cordial pairing and timings that line up.
Lazy: an ingredient list without quantities.
Pitfall: alcohol pairings for someone who does not drink.
