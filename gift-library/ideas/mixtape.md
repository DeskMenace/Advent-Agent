# The Mixtape of Your Year

A cassette in a hand-labelled case. Put it in the deck, scrub the tape with your finger, flip to Side B. Each track is a short original tune named after a moment of their year, with liner notes.

## Make it theirs

- Track titles and liner notes are moments from their year.
- The J-card is designed in their taste.

## Great vs lazy

**Great:** Tape hiss, wow and flutter, reels that spin at the right speed; a hidden track.

**Lazy:** A list of real song titles (never include copyrighted songs or lyrics).
