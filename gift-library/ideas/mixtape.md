# The Mixtape of Your Year

A cassette in a hand-labelled case. Put it in the deck, scrub the tape with your finger, flip it over. Side A is original tunes named after moments of their year, with liner notes. Side B is a real playlist the agent curated for them, ready to take into Spotify or any music app.

*Everything below is examples to spark ideas, not requirements. Stay in this idea, bend it, or build something new in the same family.*

## Make it theirs

- Track titles and liner notes are moments from their year.
- Side B: real songs chosen for those moments (titles and artists only, never lyrics or audio).
- The J-card is designed in their taste.

## Make it useful (offer in your note)

- Side A in Spotify: the mp3s can be added as Local Files in the Spotify desktop app and put in a playlist, which syncs to their phone
- Side B in Spotify: a text list they can paste into a playlist-import tool, plus a search link per song; if their agent has a Spotify connection, offer to create the playlist for them
- A printable J-card

## Great vs lazy

**Great:** Tape hiss, wow and flutter, reels that spin at the right speed; a hidden track.

**Lazy:** A list of song titles with no reason for each, or lyrics copied from real songs (never allowed).
