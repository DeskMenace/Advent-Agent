Verified means the agent can point at where it saw it. Small and true beats big and fuzzy.
Great: a paper scrapbook with taped-in "photos" (SVG) of the bookshelf built, the 52 Mondays run, the bug closed after a year, each dated.
Lazy: "You achieved so much this year!" with generic icons.
Pitfall: counting things the recipient does not consider accomplishments.
