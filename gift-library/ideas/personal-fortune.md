# Their Personal Fortune Cookie

A `fortune`-style command that shows a line from their life each time they open a terminal: sayings, in-jokes, advice from their pet, small encouragements.

*Everything below is examples to spark ideas, not requirements. Stay in this idea, bend it, or build something new in the same family.*

## Make it theirs

- At least 50 lines, all specific to them; a few rare ones (1 in 100).

## Make it useful (offer in your note)

- Also available as a browser new-tab page

## Great vs lazy

**Great:** The rare fortunes are worth screenshotting.

**Lazy:** Generic motivational quotes.
