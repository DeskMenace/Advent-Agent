The joke must survive being seen every morning. Simple, bold, few words.
Great: a two-colour mug design of the recipient's catchphrase in vintage diner lettering, shown on a mockup, with an optional "if you want it printed" page listing a vendor, price and date checked; the recipient orders.
Lazy: a wall of text on a white rectangle.
Pitfall: implying the mug is on its way. The design is the gift; any order is the recipient's choice.
