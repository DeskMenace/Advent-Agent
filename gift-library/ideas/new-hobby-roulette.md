Each wedge should feel plausible for this person next Saturday.
Great: a wooden wheel with ten wedges from bird-watching to bread, each opening a card with "first hour", "what you need" and "cost: under 20".
Lazy: a list of hobbies with a random button.
Pitfall: hobbies that quietly require buying expensive gear.
