# A Song About Your Project

An anthem, rap or ballad about one of their projects: how it started, the bugs, the 2 am fixes, the launch. Karaoke screen with lyrics in time, plus the render-it-yourself sheet.

*Everything below is examples to spark ideas, not requirements. Stay in this idea, bend it, or build something new in the same family.*

## Make it theirs

- One real project, with real names, milestones and in-jokes (commit messages, a famous bug, the first user).
- The genre matches the project's mood: a hype rap for a launch, a ballad for an abandoned side project, a shanty for a long refactor.

## Make it useful (offer in your note)

- A launch-video or demo soundtrack; a ringtone

## Great vs lazy

**Great:** Real rhyme and rhythm (for rap, the syllables land on the beat), lines only their team would get, a drop or key change.

**Lazy:** Generic lyrics about 'coding all night'.
