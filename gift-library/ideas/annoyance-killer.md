Useful is the delight here; make it feel like a gift with a note that names the annoyance.
Great: a one-page tool that reformats the recipient's weekly export into the table they rebuild by hand every Monday, with paste-in and copy-out.
Lazy: a to-do list app.
Pitfall: modifying anything outside the gift; proposals only, as diffs.
