# The Puzzle Box

A Japanese-style wooden puzzle box with hidden panels and a sequence they must find. Inside, something small and lovely.

*Everything below is examples to spark ideas, not requirements. Stay in this idea, bend it, or build something new in the same family.*

## Make it theirs

- The key sequence relates to them (a date, a summit height).
- The prize inside is personal.

## Make it useful (offer in your note)

- An editable version they can fill with their own message for someone else

## Great vs lazy

**Great:** Wooden clicks, a "you'll never…" hint in their pet's voice.

**Lazy:** A password field.
