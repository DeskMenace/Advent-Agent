Trailer grammar: slow open, rising music, hard cuts, a title card, a date.
Great: canvas-rendered scenes of the recipient's allotment as an epic saga, booming synth score, "This spring… the tomatoes return."
Lazy: a slideshow with Ken Burns and no sound.
Pitfall: promising video and delivering stills without saying so.
