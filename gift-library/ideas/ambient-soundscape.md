Procedural layers (noise-based rain, crackle bursts, wind swells) avoid audible loop seams.
Great: a cabin scene with fire, snow and distant wind as layers, each with a slider, a sleep timer and a drawing that dims at night.
Lazy: one short sample looping with a click.
Pitfall: autoplay or a sudden full-volume start; fade in on the first tap.
