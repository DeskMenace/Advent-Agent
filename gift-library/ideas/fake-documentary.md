group: A  # A–H as in the bank

Earnestness is the joke. Narrate the mundane as if it mattered to history.
Great: a storyboard with hushed narration over the recipient's sourdough starter, interviews with a jar and a spatula, and archival "footage" in sepia SVG.
Lazy: a script that only says "this person is great" for three pages.
Pitfall: promising a video and delivering a script; label the form honestly.
