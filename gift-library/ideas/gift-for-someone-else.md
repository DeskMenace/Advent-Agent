id: gift-for-someone-else
title: The Gift for Someone Else
group: H  # A–H as in the bank
summary: Thoughtful gift idea for a known recipient or pet; protect third-party privacy.
suitable_if: A known recipient (partner, friend, child, pet) is explicitly in scope and their privacy can be protected; the idea stays modest and verified.
avoid_if: The third party did not come up willingly in context; the idea would reveal private facts about them; it would be awkward for the recipient to give.
forms: [real-world-proposal, printable-card, letter]
minimum_done: A thoughtful gift idea for the named person with reasons, a verified item or experience with price and date checked when applicable, and what is left for the recipient; third-party details kept to what the recipient shared.
personalization_hooks: [people the recipient mentioned wanting to treat, their pet, upcoming occasions]
skills: [gift-director, concierge, storyteller]
effort: low  # low | medium | high (token band hint only)
real_world: suggest  # none | suggest (agent never purchases)
quality_checks: [opens, content-complete, no-private-info, links-verified, price-date-checked, no-purchase-claim]
variations:
  - A makeable gift the recipient can craft for them
  - A gift idea plus a card draft to go with it
status: candidate  # candidate | approved
---
Protect the third party: use only what the recipient said about them, nothing inferred.
Great: "For your sister who loves night skies: a specific star-chart print from a named shop, price checked today, and a short card draft."
Lazy: "Get your friend a candle."
Pitfall: digging up information about the third party.
