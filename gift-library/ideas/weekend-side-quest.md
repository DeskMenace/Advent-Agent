Quest framing (objective, loot, side objectives) makes a small errand feel like play.
Great: "Objective: find the best cinnamon bun within 2 km. Loot: a ranked scorecard. Bonus: photograph one good dog." with a printable scorecard.
Lazy: "Do something fun this weekend."
Pitfall: quests that require spending or travel the recipient did not sign up for.
