Obscure, not random: the object should make the recipient say "how did you know this exists?"
Great: a hand-cranked pencil sharpener shaped like a lighthouse for the lighthouse fan, from a named shop, price and stock checked on a stated date.
Lazy: "A cool gadget" with a search link.
Pitfall: writing "ordered" anywhere; the recipient buys.
