# Snow Globe of Your Year

A glass snow globe on a little wooden base. Shake it (drag, swipe or shake the phone) and tiny objects from their year swirl with the snow, then settle into a scene.

*Everything below is examples to spark ideas, not requirements. Stay in this idea, bend it, or build something new in the same family.*

## Make it theirs

- The scene inside is something they love or know well. Examples: their dog asleep by a fire, their city, a city they dream of, a monument, their mountain, a season they love, their desk.
- Objects from their year swirl with the snow (for example the dog's ball, the laptop, the ice axe, the K-drama remote), drawn simply in the same style.
- The plaque on the base carries their name and a one-line title for their year.
- A hidden layer, for example: shake hard three times and one object stays floating with a tiny caption.

## Make it useful (offer in your note)

- A desktop snow globe that sits in a corner of the screen and snows when shaken
- A terminal snowfall where their objects drift down as ASCII
- The settled scene as a phone wallpaper

## Great vs lazy

**Great:** Glass has a highlight and slight refraction, snow drifts with inertia, objects tumble at different speeds and settle where they belong in the scene. The plaque title made them laugh.

**Lazy:** A static picture of a snow globe with their name on it.
