Specific squares are everything: "rebases at 23:50" beats "writes code".
Great: squares drawn from real patterns, a confetti burst on bingo and a print view that fits one A4/Letter page.
Lazy: 24 squares copied from an internet "developer bingo".
Pitfall: squares that name colleagues.
