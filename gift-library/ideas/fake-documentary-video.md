This is the expensive sibling of the script idea: only pick it when it can actually render well.
Great: a 60-second canvas-animated nature documentary about the office plant, captioned narration, ambient score, credits that thank the watering can.
Lazy: a video player around a static image with text.
Pitfall: blowing the whole budget on rendering and leaving no room to test playback.
