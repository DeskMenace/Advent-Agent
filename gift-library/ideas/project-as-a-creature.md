Let the project's real traits drive the anatomy: many dependencies become many legs.
Great: a friendly moss-covered beast for the allotment project, with a field-guide entry about its seasonal hibernation.
Lazy: a generic monster with the project name underneath.
Pitfall: making the creature a critique of the project.
