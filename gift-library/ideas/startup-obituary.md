Write it in the voice of a small-town newspaper: affectionate, specific, a little formal.
Great: "Survived by three forks and a Slack channel nobody leaves", with real milestones and a line about what the project taught its founder.
Lazy: a template obituary with the project name swapped in.
Pitfall: joking about a failure the recipient has not made peace with; when in doubt, choose a different idea.
