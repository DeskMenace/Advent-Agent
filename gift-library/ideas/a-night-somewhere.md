Research like a careful friend: season, access, weather, price and the honest "you book this yourself".
Great: a named hut, the route with timings, the winter-opening status, price per night checked on a stated date, the reservation link and a sketch of the view.
Lazy: "Treat yourself to a weekend away!"
Pitfall: implying anything was reserved; the page says only what was prepared.
