# Governance

## Who decides

Advent Agent has a single maintainer: Noémie Rakotomalala ([@suchipizza](https://github.com/suchipizza)). She has the final say on scope, roadmap, which ideas are approved for the gift library, and what is merged.

Contributions are welcome and discussed in the open. A maintainer decision is not a judgement of your work: some good ideas are out of scope (see the non-negotiables in `CONTRIBUTING.md`).

## A seasonal project

This is a seasonal project. It is busiest from November to December, when the calendar is in use. After December expect slow responses, sometimes weeks. Issues and pull requests are not forgotten, they wait.

## How decisions are recorded

Product and architecture decisions are written down, with a date, in [`docs/handoff/DECISIONS.md`](docs/handoff/DECISIONS.md). If your change needs a new decision, propose it in your pull request by adding an entry there. The most recent entry wins over older text.

## Forks

The code is MIT and the gift library is CC BY 4.0, so you are free to fork and take the project in another direction. Please read `TRADEMARKS.md` first about names.
