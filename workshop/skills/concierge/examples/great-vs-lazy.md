# Concierge: great vs lazy

## Product
- **Great:** One exact item (name, colour, size left open with a size guide link), vendor page opened today, price 34.90 CHF with `checkedAt`, delivery cutoff for Christmas, a fallback if sold out, "Open the link, choose your size, pay. Nothing has been ordered or booked.", and a two-sentence reason tied to a shared hobby.
- **Lazy:** "A nice scarf from any shop, around 30 bucks", no link, no date, or worse: "I ordered you a scarf, it arrives Thursday."

## Book
- **Great:** The exact edition in the recipient's language, ISBN visible on the page, price and currency, why this book and not the obvious one, a library alternative, "Nothing has been ordered."
- **Lazy:** A bestseller from memory with a dead link and a price from two years ago.

## Experience
- **Great:** A concert or workshop with a real date in January, availability seen on the page, price per person for one (no partner assumed), how to get there by public transport, cancellation terms, what's left: "Pick the date, pay, add it to your calendar."
- **Lazy:** "Go see a show sometime!" or "I reserved two seats for you."

## Hut night
- **Great:** A specific mountain hut with its official page, the room type, the price per night incl. half board, the dates the hut page shows as open, the approach and its difficulty, gear notes, a ceiling check, "Nothing has been booked."
- **Lazy:** A hut that is closed in winter, no price, and "I booked it for you".
