# Sound Studio: great vs lazy

## Music box
- **Great:** 24 bars in G major, 3/4, melody mostly stepwise with one leap to the high point in bar 17, bass roots on beat 1, loops cleanly, WAV exported, player with keyboard support, no autoplay, a sheet with lyrics-free style notes.
- **Lazy:** Random notes from a scale array every beat, no phrase, no ending, audio starts on page load.

## Song
- **Great:** Verse/chorus/verse/chorus/outro, 96 seconds, lyrics one syllable per note with the chorus repeated exactly, pluck plus bass plus light hat, lyric line in the player, optional robot rendition labelled as such, WAV plus a complete render-it-yourself sheet with a 90-word prompt and a short variant. No provider named.
- **Lazy:** Lyrics pasted as a paragraph with no alignment, a single pad holding one chord, no WAV, a sheet that says "use <a named provider> to make this".

## Jingle / theme
- **Great:** A 6-bar theme in the game's title screen, triggered after the first tap, reused as a 2-bar win sting, mute toggle.
- **Lazy:** An 8-second loop that plays on load and cannot be stopped.

## Soundscape
- **Great:** Rain, distant wind and a fireplace with sliders, a "Winter evening" preset, a soft music-box motif every 40 seconds, Play/Stop, remembers slider positions in memory.
- **Lazy:** White noise at full volume with no sliders and no stop.
