# Sound Studio: great vs lazy

## Music box
- **Great:** A little box you crank by dragging in circles; 24 bars in G major, 3/4, tempo follows the crank, one leap to the high point in bar 17; opening the lid reveals a tiny dancer shaped like their cat. WAV exported, mute, no autoplay. Emotion `cute`, interaction `crank`.
- **Lazy:** Random notes from a scale array every beat, no phrase, no ending, audio starts on page load.

## Karaoke song
- **Great:** A deadpan power ballad about their sourdough starter: verse/chorus/verse/chorus/outro, 96 seconds, one syllable per note, chorus repeated exactly; the karaoke screen highlights each word as it sounds; mute always visible; optional robot rendition labelled as such; WAV plus a complete render-it-yourself sheet. The share frame is the chorus line lit up. No provider named. Emotion `ridiculous`.
- **Lazy:** An audio file with a play button and the lyrics pasted as a paragraph below, no timing, no mute, and a sheet that says "use <a named provider> to make this".

## Mixtape
- **Great:** A cassette with their year's "greatest hits" (six 15-second themes named after real moments they shared), fake liner notes, scrub the tape with a finger, flip for Side B; the share frame is the J-card tracklist.
- **Lazy:** Six identical chord loops with track names like "Song 1".

## Jingle / theme
- **Great:** A 6-bar theme in the game's title screen, triggered after the first tap, reused as a 2-bar win sting, mute toggle.
- **Lazy:** An 8-second loop that plays on load and cannot be stopped.

## Soundscape
- **Great:** Their hut on a snowy night: rain, wind and a fireplace with sliders you drag like stove knobs, a "Winter evening" preset, a soft music-box motif every 40 seconds, Play/Stop; the share frame is the illustrated hut with the window glowing.
- **Lazy:** White noise at full volume with no sliders and no stop.
