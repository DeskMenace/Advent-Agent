# Render-it-yourself sheet (skeleton)

Copy, fill, save as `assets/render-it-yourself.md`. Keep the headings; the calendar renders each block with a copy button. Name no provider anywhere.

```markdown
# <Song title>

<One line: what the song is and who it is for, in the recipient's language.>

## Lyrics

[Verse 1]
...

[Chorus]
...

[Verse 2]
...

[Chorus]
...

[Outro]
...

## Style

- Tags: <genre>, <instruments>, <era or texture>, <two adjectives>
- Tempo: <BPM> · Key: <key> · Time: <4/4>
- Mood: <three words>
- Vocals: <range, tone, delivery; or "instrumental">

## Ready-to-paste prompt

<One paragraph, 60–120 words: genre, instruments, tempo, key, mood, structure (verse/chorus), vocal description, production notes (warm, lo-fi, close-miked, wide), and "lyrics as written below". Then the full lyrics again or a reference to them.>

## Short prompt

<Under 200 characters: style, tempo, mood, vocals.>

---
Paste this into the music generator of your choice to hear a full production. The version in the gift is the music-box original, written for you.
```
