# Budget tiers

The calendar passes a **budget tier** in the day brief. It comes from the user's "How generous is your Secret Santa?" setting, and from the model and mode in use. Token ranges are advisory: they include reading the brief, the skills and the kit, building, testing and fixing.

| Tier | Advisory tokens per gift | Who usually gets it | What it buys |
|---|---|---|---|
| **S** | up to ~30k | "Light" setting, small or fast models | One page, one mechanic, kit blocks inlined, little or no iteration |
| **M** | ~30k–100k | "Standard" setting (default) | 2–3 interactions, personalised illustration, sound, one round of testing and fixing |
| **L** | ~100k–300k | "Generous" setting | Several scenes, custom art, physics, an export pipeline, careful testing |
| **XL** | 3× the daily budget, at least L | Day 1 (at setup) and December 24 | A complete, polished experience |

## Choosing inside your tier

- **Pick the idea's `tier` (its minimum) at or below yours.** An idea marked `tier: L` can be shrunk to M by dropping scenes, never by dropping polish.
- **Quality is non-negotiable; scope is negotiable.** Cut features before you cut finish: easing, typography, sound design, the share frame, the note.
- **Reuse the kit.** It exists so a tier S gift can still look expensive. A pop-up card or jelly fruit from the kit, deeply personalised, is a good S gift.
- **Know your strengths.** If you are weaker at visual design, choose ideas whose delight comes from writing, timing or a game mechanic. If you can't run a browser to test, prefer simpler interactions you can reason about.

## What users are told

The README and FAQ say plainly that gift size depends on the agent and the budget: *"A Secret Santa running on a small budget or a smaller model makes smaller gifts. Give it more room in Settings, or use a stronger agent, if you want more elaborate ones."*
