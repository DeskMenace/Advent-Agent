# Storyteller: great vs lazy

## Card
- **Great:** A pop-up card that opens in 3D: inside, their bike stands up from the fold with a tiny bell you can ring. Three sentences about the hour they usually open doors ("6:40, before coffee"). The capture hook opens the card; the salutation is marked `data-advent-private`. Emotion `cute`, interaction `open`.
- **Lazy:** A flat image of a tree with a 12-line poem printed on it and "Merry Christmas! Hope you have a wonderful day!". Nothing to touch, nothing to share.

## Letter
- **Great:** A sealed envelope you tear along a perforation; the letter is from their kettle, 250 words, one concrete detail per paragraph, a postscript hidden under the stamp. Emotion `absurd`.
- **Lazy:** 900 words of general encouragement on a scrolling page, three uses of "journey".

## Poem in a form
- **Great:** A villanelle whose refrains are checked for meter, built around the motif from door 3, revealed line by line as they scratch frost off a window. The form holds on every line.
- **Lazy:** "A villanelle" with 14 lines, no refrains, rhymes "snow/go/glow/know" four times, centred on a gradient.

## Short story
- **Great:** 900 words, one scene in a night tram; each stop is a page turn, and the last stop is a tiny map with their street (blurred in the share frame).
- **Lazy:** A 3,000-word fantasy prologue that ends on "To be continued" with no second part planned.

## Zine
- **Great:** 8 pages on "how to fold this zine" that becomes a small meditation on patience; page 5 has a pull-tab; page order checked against the fold; the print file is in `downloads`.
- **Lazy:** Eight blocks of text in a grid with no fold order, pages 7 and 8 empty.

## Deadpan document
- **Great:** "Certificate of Advanced Procrastination, Class II", issued by the "Bureau of Unfinished Tuesdays", played completely straight; they press and hold to stamp the seal, which lands slightly crooked. The share frame is the stamped certificate.
- **Lazy:** A certificate with a real university's name as issuer and the person's full name copied from a folder.

## Interactive fiction
- **Great:** 14 nodes, 3 endings, choices that change the inventory, undo and restart, keyboard navigable, finishable in 6 minutes, every path tested; the share frame is the ending they found.
- **Lazy:** 4 nodes, two choices that lead to the same text, one path throws because a node id is misspelled.
