# Storyteller: great vs lazy

## Card
- **Great:** Front: one generative snowflake (seeded by the day), the words "Door 6". Inside: three sentences about the exact hour the recipient usually opens doors (from feedback: "opened at 6:40 before coffee"), ending with a line that lands. Print CSS tested.
- **Lazy:** Clip-art tree, "Merry Christmas! Hope you have a wonderful day!", no inside, no print rules.

## Letter
- **Great:** A letter from the recipient's future January self, 300 words, one concrete detail per paragraph drawn from shared hobbies, one gentle joke at the end, signed with a wink. Readable in two minutes.
- **Lazy:** 900 words of general encouragement that would fit anyone, three uses of "journey".

## Poem in a form
- **Great:** A villanelle whose two refrains are checked for meter, built around the motif from door 3 (declared in `continuity`). The form holds on every line.
- **Lazy:** "A villanelle" with 14 lines, no refrains, rhymes "snow/go/glow/know" four times.

## Short story
- **Great:** 900 words, one scene in a night tram, one turn at the end, a nod to the recipient's language. No name of a real person.
- **Lazy:** A 3,000-word fantasy prologue that ends on "To be continued" with no second part planned.

## Zine
- **Great:** 8 pages on "how to fold this zine" that becomes a small meditation on patience; page order checked against the fold template; prints on one A4 sheet.
- **Lazy:** Eight blocks of text in a grid with no fold order, pages 7 and 8 empty.

## Fake document
- **Great:** "Certificate of Advanced Procrastination, Class II", issued by the "Bureau of Unfinished Tuesdays", with an absurd seal and a validity date in 2084. Clearly a joke, nicely typeset.
- **Lazy:** A certificate with a real university's name as issuer and the recipient's full real name copied from a folder.

## Interactive fiction
- **Great:** 14 nodes, 3 endings, choices that change the inventory, undo and restart, keyboard navigable, finishable in 6 minutes. Tested every path.
- **Lazy:** 4 nodes, two choices that lead to the same text, one path throws because a node id is misspelled.
