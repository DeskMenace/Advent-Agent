# Maker: great vs lazy

## Generator
- **Great:** "Sourdough name generator" (shared hobby: baking): pull a little brass lever, the jar wobbles and a seeded name pops out on a label; a "keep this one" shelf stored locally with export; the share frame is their favourite label on the jar. Emotion `funny`, interaction `pull`.
- **Lazy:** `Math.random()` picking from 10 words into a plain text box, no copy, no state, a page that breaks under 400 px.

## Mini-tool
- **Great:** A knitting gauge converter with verified formulas, a dial you turn instead of a number field, results that update live, an "about the maths" fold-out, state remembered locally with a visible "stays on this device" line.
- **Lazy:** A converter with one factor wrong and an alert() on invalid input.

## Dashboard
- **Great:** The person shared a reading log in an allowed folder: the gift embeds the titles only (no notes, no private text) as a deadpan "Annual Report of the Reading Department", with a bar per month that grows as they scroll; the share frame is the headline chart; the note says exactly what was copied.
- **Lazy:** A chart over invented data presented as theirs, or a tool that reads their folder live and writes a "cleaned" file back into it.

## Cheat sheet
- **Great:** One A4 page of the shortcuts they said they forget, grouped, big keys, a blank row for their own, print file in `downloads`; on screen, pressing a listed shortcut lights its row.
- **Lazy:** A wall of text copied from documentation, three pages when printed, keys and descriptions misaligned.

## Tracker
- **Great:** A January habit grid, one tap per cell stamps a tiny snowflake, stored locally, export as JSON, a streak counter that does not shame ("3 days, keep going").
- **Lazy:** A grid that loses everything on reload because `localStorage` threw on the opaque origin and nobody caught it.
