# Arcade: great vs lazy

## Action toy
- **Great:** "Catch the sausages": their husky (named in memory) runs under a falling-sausage sky; one mechanic, speed rises, at 24 points the dog does a tiny happy spin nobody announced. Escape pauses, R restarts, swipe on touch, mute, particles off under reduced motion. The share frame is the score card with the dog's portrait. Emotion `ridiculous`, interaction `catch`.
- **Lazy:** A generic platformer with jump, double jump and three enemy types, "Happy holidays Sam" on the title screen; the second enemy throws an error and there is no touch control.

## Puzzle
- **Great:** A 4×4 sliding puzzle of a drawing of their favourite mountain, shuffled by a seeded solvable permutation (parity checked), move counter, an "I give up" button that solves it with an animation. The solved picture is the share frame.
- **Lazy:** Random shuffle that is unsolvable half the time, no counter, no way out.

## Quiz
- **Great:** "How well do you know your own December?" Ten questions about earlier doors and shared hobbies, each wrong answer funny on its own, three result tiers with a printable verdict card as the share frame.
- **Lazy:** Ten trivia questions copied from general knowledge, results screen says "You scored 7/10".

## Crossword / word search
- **Great:** 12 words from their year, clues written as small riddles, "check" and "reveal a letter", a completed-grid share frame with the theme word lit up.
- **Lazy:** A word search with 30 generic words, several unplaced words silently dropped, no way to check.

## Escape room
- **Great:** Their actual workshop as one room, four locks (code, riddle, sequence, colour order), every clue in the illustration, hints after 60 seconds, answers normalised, a satisfying door animation and a sentence at the end.
- **Lazy:** A text input that compares `===` with "Snow", no hints, and the page reloads on a wrong answer.

## Tactile toy
- **Great:** A snow globe holding their city's tram stop: shake by dragging, tilting or pressing Space, snow with inertia that settles slowly, a second shake reveals a tiny figure waving. The capture hook freezes it mid-swirl; the loop is 6 seconds of shake and settle. Emotion `touching`.
- **Lazy:** A `requestAnimationFrame` loop that spawns confetti forever, no pause, no reduced-motion check, nothing to touch.
