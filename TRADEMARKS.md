# Trademarks and brand assets

The MIT licence covers the code. The CC BY 4.0 licence covers the gift library. Neither licence covers the names and artwork below.

**Not licensed under MIT or CC BY:**

- The names "Advent Agent" and "Desk Menace".
- Gary: his name, his artwork, lines and identity. Gary is covered by the Gary Asset Licence 1.0 (see `THIRD_PARTY.md`).
- The logos and the Desk Menace branding.

**What this means**

- If you modify the code and publish your fork or package, give it a different name. Do not call it "Advent Agent", and do not use "Desk Menace" or Gary in a way that suggests the project or its maintainer made or endorsed your version.
- You may say, accurately, that your project is a fork of or is based on Advent Agent.
- Forks may remove the Desk Menace credit and Gary. They stay in this upstream project.

**What stays free**

The MIT code and the CC BY gift library stay free to use, change, fork and redistribute, including commercially. This file does not take that back. It only protects the names and artwork.
